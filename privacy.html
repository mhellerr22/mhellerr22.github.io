ClipWatch privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026. Back to the ClipWatch home page.

Who this applies to

ClipWatch is a personal tool used only by its developer, on the developer's own computer and for the developer's own Google account. It is not offered to the public and nobody else signs in with it. This policy describes what the tool does with the Google account it is connected to.

What Google data ClipWatch accesses

When its owner signs in, ClipWatch asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file ("See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app"). With it, ClipWatch:

  • creates one Google Sheets workbook in the signed-in account's own Google Drive;
  • writes campaign information, collected from public web pages, into that workbook;
  • reads back the few cells its owner types into the workbook, such as links and yes/no answers;
  • reads the email address of the signed-in account, only to check that the correct account is being used.

It can open only files it created itself. It does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Photos, or any other file in the account.

How the data is used

Only to keep that workbook up to date and to let its owner record answers in it. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored and who can see it

The sign-in token and ClipWatch's database are stored on the owner's computer, in a folder outside the project. ClipWatch has no server of its own, and the developer receives no data from it. Information read from Google is not sold and is not used for advertising. ClipWatch does not share it with other people or companies; the owner may use parts of their own information, such as a link or a deadline, in automation tools that the owner runs and controls.

Retention and deletion

The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. At any time the owner can revoke ClipWatch's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, delete the workbook from Google Drive, and delete ClipWatch's local folder. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ClipWatch's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. Questions can be sent to the developer through GitHub: github.com/mhellerr22.
